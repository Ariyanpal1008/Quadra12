Quadra 12 — Class 12 CBSE Tracker, Examination Hall & 12th Hackers

An independent, free Class 12 CBSE PCMB study workspace covering Physics (042), Chemistry (043), Biology (044), and Mathematics (041), with Light & Dark mode, mobile top menu (☰), Help Desk, and community Suggestion Box.

Core Study Workspace

  • Overview Dashboard, Live Board Clock & Achievements: Features a live Apple-style CBSE Class 12 Board Exam Countdown Clock (targeting Feb 16, 10:30 AM IST with automatic annual rollover 2027 → 2028 and custom date support), a gamified Achievements gallery of 9 milestone badges (such as Quick Learner for completing 3–5 chapters in one day and Exam Ready for scoring >90% on a sample paper), combined syllabus mastery across all 50 NCERT chapters (200 completion checkpoints), subjects completed out of 4, CBSE sample papers attempted, and 12th Hackers lectures watched, plus subject cards with quick stage toggles and 100% completion shimmer.
  • Subject Tracker: Tracks all 50 NCERT chapters — Physics (14), Chemistry (10), Biology (13), and Mathematics (13) — across 4 checkpoints per chapter (Lecture done, Practice done, NCERT done, PYQ done), one-click subject bulk toggles, and 50 interactive custom SVG study diagrams.
  • Examination Hall: Practice official CBSE 2026–27 Sample Question Paper PDFs (70 marks PCB / 80 marks Maths) with an embedded PDF reader, offline download, 3-hour (03:00:00) countdown timer, 33/38-question OMR & written checklist, automatic Section A grading, and personal best score tracking.
  • 12th Hackers One-Shot Lectures: Stream all 50 official chapter lectures with unit notes, high-yield board topics, and key formulas, bidirectionally synced to Lecture done in Subject Tracker. Includes 3 player embed modes (Standard, Pure Embed, No-Cookie), auto-completion detection, and an Open on YouTube + Mark Done & Next fallback bar.

Help Desk, Suggestion Box & Creator Inbox

  • Help Desk Guide: Accessible from the top mobile menu (☰) and footer; explains how every tab works (Overview, Subject Tracker, Examination Hall, and 12th Hackers) with direct jump buttons.
  • Suggest Addition / Change (Suggestion Box): Visitors can propose new features, design changes, syllabus corrections, or bug reports for any tab. Supports Google Sign-In cloud tracking (Submitted, Planned, Added / Updated with creator replies via Firebase Firestore) as well as a direct Email Creator button (ariyanpal090@gmail.com).
  • Creator Inbox: Moderation inbox unlocked for the verified creator to review visitor requests, update statuses, and leave reply notes.
  • Theme & Data Privacy: Switch anytime between Light and Dark modes. All chapter checkboxes, best exam scores, and theme choices save automatically in local browser storage without requiring sign-in.
