Quadra 12 — Class 12 CBSE Tracker, Examination Hall & 12th Hackers

An independent Class 12 CBSE PCMB study workspace covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics.

Workspace Features

  • Overview: Combined progress across all 50 NCERT Class 12 chapters (200 completion checkpoints).
  • Subject Tracker: Track Lecture, Practice, NCERT, and PYQ completion for Physics (14 chapters), Chemistry (10 chapters), Biology (13 chapters), and Mathematics (13 chapters), with custom SVG diagrams.
  • Examination Hall: Practice official CBSE 2026–27 Sample Question Paper PDFs with a 3-hour countdown timer, OMR checklist, and instant Section A scoring.
  • 12th Hackers Playlist: Watch all 50 one-shot chapter lectures synced directly to Subject Tracker.

About the Creator

I am a Class 12 student currently preparing for board exams.

The Problem: Keeping board exam preparation organized and finding one clean, distraction-free study tracker was a real struggle.

The Solution: I built this app to solve that exact problem for myself and other students facing the same challenge.
