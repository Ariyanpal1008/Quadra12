Quadra 12 — Class 12 CBSE Tracker, Examination Hall & 12th Hackers

An independent, free Class 12 CBSE PCMB study workspace covering Physics (042), Chemistry (043), Biology (044), and Mathematics (041), with Light & Dark mode, mobile top menu (☰), Help Desk, and community Suggestion Box.

Core Study Workspace

  • Overview Dashboard, Live Board Clock & Achievements: Features a live Apple-style CBSE Class 12 Board Exam Countdown Clock (targeting Feb 16, 10:30 AM IST with automatic annual rollover 2027 → 2028 and custom date support), a gamified Achievements gallery of 9 milestone badges (including Quick Learner, Syllabus Sprinter, and Exam Ready) with dynamic reversible unlocking/relocking, combined syllabus mastery across all 50 NCERT chapters (200 completion checkpoints), subjects completed out of 4, CBSE sample papers attempted, and 12th Hackers lectures watched.
  • Native Web Audio API Sound System: Built-in zero-dependency tactile audio feedback with a top sticky navbar mute toggle (🔊 / 🔇), soft haptic pops and reverse ticks on chapter checkboxes, mechanical clicks on theme toggles, muted taps on OMR bubbles, harmonic chimes on badge unlocks and 100% subject mastery, and an exam timer completion bell.
  • Subject Tracker: Tracks all 50 NCERT chapters — Physics (14), Chemistry (10), Biology (13), and Mathematics (13) — across 4 checkpoints per chapter (Lecture done, Practice done, NCERT done, PYQ done), one-click subject bulk toggles, and 50 interactive custom SVG study diagrams.
  • Examination Hall: Solve independently authored Class 12 PCMB mock papers (70 marks PCB / 80 marks Maths) featuring a 15-minute Reading Time timer, 47 embedded vector SVG diagrams & graphs, 100% synced downloadable Question Paper & Marking Scheme A4 PDFs, Section A OMR auto-grading, Sections B–E camera/gallery photo uploads (stored strictly in local browser IndexedDB & localStorage with automatic post-grading wipe), and Gemini 3.8 AI step-marking.
  • 12th Hackers One-Shot Lectures: Stream all 50 official chapter lectures with unit notes, high-yield board topics, and key formulas, bidirectionally synced to Lecture done in Subject Tracker. Includes 3 player embed modes (Standard, Pure Embed, No-Cookie), auto-completion detection, and an Open on YouTube + Mark Done & Next fallback bar.
  • Official Settings (v2.5 via ☰ Menu → Settings): Clean progressive-disclosure Settings hub containing 7 organized sections: Cloud Sync (account-less 6-digit code, live scannable QR code, optional camera QR scanner icon beside the 6-digit input that requests browser camera permission only on tap and auto-stops after decoding, plus offline .json backup), Audio & Tactile Feedbacks (sound test sandbox & appearance), Interactive 3D Studio, Exam Countdown & Urgency Alerts, 12th Hackers Video Player Preferences, Privacy & Reset (local storage quota meter, single-subject resets for Physics/Chemistry/Biology/Math, and double-confirmed Danger Zone Master Reset), and About & Links (Terms & Conditions · 2026 and Official GitHub Repository).
  • Interactive Three.js 3D Study Studio: Inspect real studio-lit WebGL 3D models for Moving-Coil Galvanometer (Physics), [ML₆] Octahedral Complex (Chemistry), DNA Double Helix (Biology), Vector Cross Product & Double Cone (Maths), and the Grandmaster Achievement Trophy with OrbitControls and Explode slider.
